# REFERENCE BENCHMARK

Sources reviewed:

- https://ifitness.ng/
- https://www.skyfitnessng.com/

## What both sites do well

- They make membership or joining actions easy to find in the primary navigation.
- They present classes as a key discovery path rather than an afterthought.
- They use fitness-specific trust signals such as facilities, trainers and timetable information.
- They lean on image-led storytelling to communicate scale and energy quickly.

## What must not be copied

- The section order and page hierarchy.
- The wording and slogan style.
- The visual identity.
- The layout rhythm and card treatment.
- The testimonial treatment from Sky Fitness.

## Benchmark takeaways for Home of Fitness

- Membership should be discoverable within one or two interactions from the home page.
- Class discovery should feel editorial, not list-like.
- Trainer and facility credibility should be concrete, concise and visually strong.
- The shop should be integrated as a real revenue path, not hidden in the footer.
- The interface must feel more disciplined, compact and art-directed than the reference sites.
