# Responsive Audit

## Viewports Checked
- 320px
- 390px
- 768px
- 1024px
- 1440px

## Results
- No unintended horizontal overflow was detected at the checked widths.
- The mobile menu opened and closed correctly.
- The home hero and backdrop sections now scale down more gracefully on smaller screens.
- The shop cards and gallery media no longer dominate the viewport.

## Notes
- The layout preserves readability on compact viewports after the image downsizing.
- The new size constraints keep the content moving upward naturally rather than leaving large empty gaps.
